Software and firmware for DJs and music gear. Each project has its own page; they all share one look (site-style), each in its own colour.

Track Kommander

macOS and Windows app, beside Traktor Pro 3 / 4

Finds the next track: ranks your library against what is playing and loads the one you pick, transition set up. Writes your Traktor collection to a USB stick CDJs read on their own.

OpenDownload

Optimist

Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

An open, modular firmware platform for the FM-1 (from SLOOP / Felucca), with an installer, a builder, a simulator and an editor that run in the browser.

OpenGitHub

jank

ESP32-S3 device and its web page

Ableton Link with no router: joins Optimist's MODE JANK group of FM-1s and brings in computers, MIDI and modular gear, with MIDI clock and CLOCK / RESET outputs.

OpenGitHub

DN2 Wishes

Firmware mod for the Elektron Digitone II, OS 1.12

Extra machines and effects for the Digitone II, built in the page from your own copies of Elektron's OS files. Unofficial; a fork of dn2mk2.
